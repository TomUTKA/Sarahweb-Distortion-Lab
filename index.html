🧩 What’s inside
Distortion Lab
WebGL effects: Warp, RGB Split, Pixelate, VHS, CRT, Bloom, Neon Edges, Kaleidoscope, Ripple, Light Leaks, Blocks, Tape Roll, Pixel Sort+, Monochrome, Motion Trail, Text Popups.
ASCII Generator
Live camera → ASCII with character sets (classic, numbers, latin, binary, Cyrillic, Kana, Braille), density & color controls.
Tips
Shuffle effect order to discover wild combos. On slower devices, disable Bloom or CRT for smoother performance.
Compatibility
WebGL-first, with 2D fallback. Works on modern browsers, mobile & desktop.
